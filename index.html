Who's behind this

Dashboards designed for people, not analysts

We started ClarityMetrics after seeing the same thing again and again: small businesses with plenty of data and no time to read it. Most dashboards fail not because the numbers are wrong, but because nobody can tell what they mean.

So every report we build starts from one question — what decision will you make with this? — and works backward. The automation keeps it running every week; the design makes it something you'll actually open.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is my data safe?

Your files live in a private folder only you and ClarityMetrics can access. Data is used only to produce your reports — never shared, sold, or used to train AI models. AI summaries are generated through business APIs that don't train on your data. Happy to sign an NDA before you share anything.

Do I need Power BI or any special software?

No. Your report arrives by email as a PDF, and your dashboard opens in a web browser. If your team already uses Microsoft 365 and Power BI, we can publish straight into your workspace instead.

My data is messy. Will this still work?

Almost always. Setup includes mapping and cleaning your exports. If your history needs heavy work, we'll tell you upfront and quote a one-time cleanup sprint before you commit.

How accurate are the AI-written summaries?

The AI only explains numbers that were already calculated from your data — it doesn't invent figures. Every summary is checked automatically against the source numbers, and anything unusual is reviewed by a person before it goes out.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime before your next billing date. The one-time setup fee covers the build and isn't refundable once your dashboard is delivered.

I run an agency. Can I resell this to my clients?

Yes — the Executive plan is fully white-labeled with your logo and colors. Ask about partner pricing if you'd like dashboards for three or more clients.
